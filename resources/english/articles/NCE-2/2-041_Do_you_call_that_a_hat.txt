'Do you call that a hat?' I said to my wife. ->> “你把那个叫帽子吗？”我对妻子说。
'You needn't be so rude about it,' my wife answered as she looked at herself in the mirror. ->> “你说话没必要这样不客气，”我的妻子边回答边照着镜子。
I sat down on one of those modern chairs with holes in it and waited. ->> 我坐在一个新式的满是网眼儿的椅子上，等待着。
We had been in the hat shop for half an hour and my wife was still in front of the mirror. ->> 我们在这家帽店已经呆了半个小时了，而我的妻子仍在镜子面前。
'We mustn't buy things we don't need,' I remarked suddenly. ->> “我们不应该买我们不需要的东西，”我突然发表意见说。
I regretted saying it almost at once. ->> 我几乎马上就后悔说了这句话。
'You needn't have said that,' my wife answered. ->> “你没必要这么说，”我妻子回答说。
'I needn't remind you of that terrible tie you bought yesterday.' ->> “我也不必提醒你昨天买的那条糟糕透了的领带。”
'I find it beautiful,' I said. ->> “我觉得它好看，”我说。
'A man can never have too many ties.' ->> “男人有多少领带也不会嫌多。”
'And a woman can't have too many hats,' she answered. ->> “女人有多少帽子也不嫌多。”她回答。
Ten minutes later we walked out of the shop together. ->> 10分钟以后，我们一道走出了商店。
My wife was wearing a hat that looked like a lighthouse! ->> 我妻子戴着一顶像灯塔一样的帽子。


rude ->> /rud/ adj. 无礼的  
The manager's rude comment shocked everyone in the meeting. ->> 经理的粗鲁言论震惊了会议上的每个人。  
It's considered rude to interrupt someone during a conversation. ->> 在谈话中打断别人被认为是不礼貌的。  

mirror ->> /ˈmɪrər/ n. 镜子  
Every morning, she checks her reflection in the mirror before leaving for work. ->> 每天早上，她在上班前都会对着镜子检查自己的形象。  
The antique mirror in the hallway adds a touch of elegance to the decor. ->> 走廊上的古董镜子为装饰增添了一丝优雅。  

hole ->> /hoʊl/ n. 孔  
The carpenter accidentally drilled a hole too big in the wood. ->> 木匠不小心在木头上钻了一个太大的孔。  
Her favorite sweater has a small hole near the hem, but she still loves to wear it. ->> 她最喜欢的毛衣下摆附近有一个小洞，但她仍然喜欢穿它。  

remark ->> /rɪˈmɑrk/ v. 评说  
He couldn't help but remark on how delicious the dinner tasted. ->> 他忍不住感叹这顿晚餐多么美味。  
During the lecture, the professor remarked on the importance of historical context. ->> 在讲座中，教授谈到了历史背景的重要性。  

remind ->> /riˈmaɪnd/ v. 提醒  
Please remind me to call the dentist tomorrow. ->> 请提醒我明天打电话给牙医。  
The post-it note on the fridge is there to remind you to buy milk. ->> 冰箱上的便利贴是为了提醒你买牛奶。  

lighthouse ->> /ˈlaɪˌthaʊs/ n. 灯塔  
The old lighthouse guided ships safely to shore for over a century. ->> 一个多世纪以来，古老的灯塔引导船只安全靠岸。  
Standing tall on the cliff, the lighthouse remains a symbol of hope. ->> 灯塔高高地矗立在悬崖上，至今仍然是希望的象征。
