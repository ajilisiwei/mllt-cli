My old friend, Harrison, had lived in the Mediterranean for many years before he returned to England. ->> 我的老朋友哈里森在回到英国以前曾多年居住在地中海地区。
He had often dreamed of retiring in England and had planned to settle down in the country. ->> 过去他常幻想退休后到英国，并计划在乡间安顿下来。
He had no sooner returned than he bought a house and went to live there. ->> 他刚一回到英国便买下了一幢房子住了进去。
Almost immediately he began to complain about the weather, for even though it was still summer, it rained continually and it was often bitterly cold. ->> 几乎立刻他就开始抱怨那里的天气，因为即使那时仍为夏季，但雨总是下个不停，而且常常冷得厉害。
After so many years of sunshine, Harrison got a shock. ->> 在阳光下生活了那么多年后的哈里森对此感到震惊。
He acted as if he had never lived in England before. ->> 他的举动就好像他从未在英国生活过一样。
In the end, it was more than he could bear. ->> 最后，这超出了他的承受能力。
He had hardly had time to settle down when he sold the house and left the country. ->> 他还没来得及安顿下来就卖掉了房子，离开了这个国家。
The dream he had had for so many years ended there. ->> 他多年来的幻想从此破灭。
Harrison had thought of everything except the weather. ->> 哈里森把每件事情都考虑到了，唯独没想到天气。


except /ɪkˈsɛpt/ prep. 除了  
She finished all her tasks except the last one. ->> 她完成了除了最后一项之外的所有任务。  
Everyone was present except John. ->> 除了约翰以外，所有人都在场。  

Mediterranean /ˌmɛdətərˈeɪniən/ n. 地中海  
We spent our summer vacation sailing the Mediterranean. ->> 我们在地中海航行度过了暑假。  
The Mediterranean is known for its beautiful blue waters. ->> 地中海以其美丽的蓝色海水而闻名。  

complain /kəmˈpleɪn/ v. 抱怨  
She tends to complain whenever things don't go her way. ->> 每当事情不如她所愿时，她就会抱怨。  
Customers often complain about the long waiting times. ->> 顾客经常抱怨等待时间过长。  

continually /kənˈtɪnjuəli/ adv. 不断地  
The clock ticks continually, marking the passage of time. ->> 时钟不断滴答作响，标志着时间的流逝。  
She continually updates her skills to stay competitive in her field. ->> 她不断更新自己的技能，以在自己的领域保持竞争力。  

bitterly /ˈbɪtərli/ adv. 苦涩地  
He sighed bitterly after hearing the disappointing news. ->> 听到这个令人失望的消息后，他痛苦地叹了口气。  
She bitterly regretted not taking the opportunity when she had the chance. ->> 当她有机会的时候，她非常后悔没有抓住这个机会。  

sunshine /ˈsənˌʃaɪn/ n. 阳光  
She loves waking up to bright sunshine streaming through her window. ->> 她喜欢在明亮的阳光透过窗户照射进来的时候醒来。  
The garden thrives in the ample sunshine during the summer months. ->> 夏季，花园在充足的阳光下茁壮成长。
