I am an art student and I paint a lot of pictures. ->> 我是个学艺术的学生，画了很多画。  
Many people pretend that they understand modern art. ->> 有很多人装作很懂现代艺术的样子。  
They always tell you what a picture is 'about'. ->> 他们总是告诉你一幅画的“意思”是什么。  
Of course, many pictures are not 'about' anything. ->> 当然，有很多画并没有任何“意思”。  
They are just pretty patterns. ->> 它们只是一些漂亮的图案。  
We like them in the same way that we like pretty curtain material. ->> 我们喜欢它们，就像喜欢漂亮的窗帘布一样。  
I think that young children often appreciate modern pictures better than anyone else. ->> 我觉得小孩子们往往比任何人都更能欣赏现代绘画。  
They notice more. ->> 他们观察到的东西更多。  
My sister is only seven, but she always tells me whether my pictures are good or not. ->> 我的妹妹只有七岁，但她总能告诉我我的画好不好。  
She came into my room yesterday. ->> 昨天她到我房间来了。  
'What are you doing?' she asked. ->> "你在做什么？" 她问。  
'I'm hanging this picture on the wall,' I answered. ->> "我正在把这幅画挂在墙上，” 我回答道。  
'It's a new one. ->> “这是一幅新画。  
Do you like it?' She looked at it critically for a moment. ->> 你喜欢吗？” 她带着批评的眼光看了一会儿。  
'It's all right,' she said, 'but isn't it upside down?' I looked at it again. ->> “挺好的，” 她说，“不过是不是挂反了？” 我再看了一下。  
She was right! ->> 她说的对！  
It was! ->> 的确是挂反了！

The art inspires many students to pursue a creative career. ->> 艺术激励许多学生追求创造性的职业。  
Art exhibitions attract visitors from all over the world. ->> 艺术展览吸引了来自世界各地的参观者。  

critic /ˈkrɪtɪk/ n. 评论家  
The critic provided a detailed analysis of the new play. ->> 评论家对这部新剧进行了详细的分析。  
Many critics gathered to review the latest film at the festival. ->> 许多评论家聚集在一起评论电影节上的最新电影。  

paint /peɪnt/ v. 画  
She loves to paint landscapes in her free time. ->> 她闲暇时喜欢画风景。  
Painting helps him relax after a long day at work. ->> 绘画可以帮助他在漫长的一天工作后放松身心。  

pretend /priˈtɛnd/ v. 假装  
Children often pretend to be superheroes during playtime. ->> 孩子们在玩耍时经常假装自己是超级英雄。  
She decided to pretend she was uninterested to keep it a surprise. ->> 她决定假装不感兴趣，以保持惊喜。  

pattern /ˈpætərn/ n. 图案  
The designer chose a unique pattern for the fabric. ->> 设计师为面料选择了独特的图案。  
Patterns in nature often influence artists' work. ->> 自然界的模式常常影响艺术家的作品。  

curtain /ˈkərtən/ n. 窗帘，幕布  
The stage curtain rose, signaling the start of the show. ->> 舞台幕布升起，演出正式开始。  
She picked out new curtains to match the color of her room. ->> 她挑选了新窗帘来搭配她房间的颜色。  

material /məˈtɪriəl/ n. 材料  
He spent the afternoon choosing material for the new project. ->> 他花了一个下午的时间为新项目选择材料。  
Different materials are tested for durability in the lab. ->> 在实验室中测试不同材料的耐用性。  

appreciate /əˈpriʃiˌeɪt/ v. 鉴赏  
He began to appreciate classical music after taking a course. ->> 参加课程后，他开始欣赏古典音乐。  
We appreciate your feedback on our services. ->> 我们感谢您对我们服务的反馈。  

notice /ˈnoʊtɪs/ v. 注意到  
She immediately noticed the changes in the room's decor. ->> 她立刻就注意到了房间装修的变化。  
He failed to notice the sign until it was too late. ->> 他没有注意到这个标志，直到为时已晚。  

whether /ˈwɛðər/ conj. 是否  
Let me know whether you can attend the meeting. ->> 请告诉我您是否可以参加会议。  
We should go for a walk whether it’s raining or not. ->> 无论下雨与否，我们都应该去散步。  

hang /hæŋ/ v. 悬挂，吊  
They hang pictures on the wall to brighten the space. ->> 他们在墙上挂画来照亮空间。  
You can hang your coat on the rack by the door. ->> 您可以将外套挂在门边的架子上。

She critically examined each part of the project. ->> 她批判性地检查了该项目的每个部分。  
Critically assessing the situation, they made an informed decision. ->> 他们批判性地评估了形势，做出了明智的决定。

The book cover was printed upside down by mistake. ->> 书的封面不小心印反了。
