Last week I went to the theatre. ->> 上星期我去看戏。
I had a very good seat. ->> 我的座位很好。
The play was very interesting. ->> 戏很有意思。
I did not enjoy it. ->> 但我却无法欣赏。
A young man and a young woman were sitting behind me. ->> 一青年男子与一青年女子坐在我的身后。
They were talking loudly. ->> 他们大声地说着话。
I got very angry. ->> 我非常生气。
I could not hear the actors. ->> 因为我听不见演员在说什么。
I turned round. ->> 我回过头去。
I looked at the man and the woman angrily. ->> 怒视着那一男一女。
They did not pay any attention. ->> 他们却毫不理会。
In the end, I could not bear it. ->> 最后，我忍不住了。
I turned round again. ‘I can't hear a word!' I said angrily. ‘It's none of your business,' the young man said rudely. ‘This is a private conversation!' ->> 我又一次回过头去，生气地说：“我一个字也听不见了！”“不关你的事，”那男的毫不客气地说，“这是私人间的谈话！”


go to the theatre ->> 去看戏。  
After dinner we decided to go to the theatre instead of watching television at home. ->> 晚饭后我们决定去看戏，而不是在家看电视。
Tourists flock to London every summer just to go to the theatre and catch a famous musical. ->> 每年夏天游客蜂拥到伦敦，只为进剧院看一场著名的音乐剧。

got angry ->> 生气。  
She got angry when the waiter brought cold soup for the third time. ->> 服务员第三次端来冷汤时，她火了。
He rarely got angry, but the unfair remark made him slam the notebook shut. ->> 他很少生气，但那句不公平的话让他啪地合上了笔记本。

turn round ->> 转身，也可用turn around。  
Hearing her name whispered, Lena turned round to find her brother grinning behind her. ->> 听到有人低声叫她的名字，莉娜转身看到哥哥正站在身后咧嘴笑。
The cyclist turned round to check for traffic before crossing the lane. ->> 骑车的人在穿过车道前转身查看是否有车辆。

pay attention ->> 注意。  
Please pay attention when the guide explains the evacuation plan. ->> 导游讲解疏散方案时请注意听。
Drivers must pay attention to the flashing lights near the school gate. ->> 司机必须注意校门附近的闪烁警示灯。

I could not bear it ->> 我无法忍受。  
The humidity was so heavy that I could not bear it after five minutes in the kitchen. ->> 厨房里湿气太重，我待了五分钟就受不了了。
I could not bear it when the movie dragged on for another tedious hour. ->> 电影又拖沓了一小时，我实在忍受不了。

none of your business ->> 不关你的事。   
When he kept asking about my salary, I told him it was none of his business. ->> 他不停地问我的薪水，我告诉他这不关他的事。
She whispered that where she was going after work was none of their business. ->> 她悄声说她下班去哪儿不关他们的事。
