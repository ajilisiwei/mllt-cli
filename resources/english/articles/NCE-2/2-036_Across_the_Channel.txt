record /ˈrɛkərd/ n. 记录  
She broke the record for the fastest marathon time. ->> 她打破了马拉松最快成绩记录。  
Keeping a detailed record is crucial for tracking progress. ->> 保留详细记录对于跟踪进度至关重要。  

strong /strɔŋ/ adj. 强壮的  
His strong determination helped him overcome many obstacles. ->> 他坚强的决心帮助他克服了许多障碍。  
The coffee was so strong that it kept me awake all night. ->> 咖啡太浓了，让我彻夜难眠。  

swimmer /ˈswɪmər/ n. 游泳运动员  
The swimmer broke her own record in the 100m freestyle. ->> 这位游泳运动员在100米自由泳比赛中打破了她自己保持的纪录。  
He is considered the fastest swimmer on the team. ->> 他被认为是队中游泳速度最快的运动员。  

succeed /səkˈsid/ v. 成功  
If you want to succeed, you must be willing to work hard. ->> 如果你想成功，你必须愿意努力工作。  
She was determined to succeed despite the challenges. ->> 尽管面临挑战，她仍决心取得成功。  

train /treɪn/ v. 训练  
He decided to train regularly to improve his performance. ->> 他决定定期训练以提高自己的表现。  
The coach will train the team every weekday afternoon. ->> 每个工作日下午教练都会对球队进行训练。  

anxiously /ˈæŋkʃəsli/ adv. 焦急地  
She waited anxiously for the results of her exam. ->> 她焦急地等待着考试结果。  
He paced anxiously in the hallway before his job interview. ->> 面试前，他在走廊里焦急地踱步。  

intend /ɪnˈtɛnd/ v. 打算  
I intend to finish the project by next week. ->> 我打算在下周之前完成该项目。  
She didn't intend to cause any harm with her comments. ->> 她的言论无意造成任何伤害。  

solid /ˈsɑləd/ adj. 固体的，硬的  
The sculpture was carved out of a solid block of marble. ->> 该雕塑是用一块坚固的大理石雕刻而成的。  
He gave a solid performance in his first lead role. ->> 他在第一次担任主角时就表现出色。
