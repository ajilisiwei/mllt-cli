I had an amusing experience last year. ->> 去年我有过一次有趣的经历。
After I had left a small village in the south of France, I drove on to the next town. ->> 在离开法国南部的一个小村庄后，我继续驶往下一个城镇。
On the way, a young man waved to me. ->> 途中，一个青年人向我招手。
I stopped and he asked me for a lift. ->> 我把车停下，他向我请求搭车。
As soon as he had got into the car, I said good morning to him in French and he replied in the same language. ->> 他一上车，我就用法语向他问好，他也同样用法语回答我。
Apart from a few words, I do not know any French at all. ->> 除了个别几个单词外，我根本不会法语。
Neither of us spoke during the journey. ->> 旅途中我们谁也没讲话。
I had nearly reached the town, when the young man suddenly said, very slowly, 'Do you speak English?' As I soon learnt, he was English himself! ->> 就要到达那个城镇时，那青年突然开口，慢慢地说道：“你会讲英语吗？”我很快了解到，他自己就是个英国人！


I /aɪ/ drove on the next town. 我继续是驶往下一个城镇。句中的副词 on 有“继续地”，“不停顿地”意思。  
I drove on to the next town to find a mechanic who could fix the flat tire. ->> 我开车前往下一个城镇寻找可以修理漏气轮胎的机械师。  
After refuelling at the village, I drove on to the next town before nightfall. ->> 在村里加油后，我在天黑前驱车前往下一个城镇。  

on /ɔn/ the way, 在途中。  
On the way to the coast we stopped for strawberries from a roadside stall. ->> 在去海岸的路上，我们停下来从路边摊买草莓。  
I finished the novel on the way home because the train ride was so long. ->> 我在回家的路上读完了这本小说，因为坐火车的时间太长了。  

ask /æsk/ for a lift, 要求搭车。  
He asked for a lift to the station because his bike chain snapped. ->> 他要求搭车去车站，因为他的自行车链条断了。  
Stranded in the rain, she asked for a lift from the passing delivery van. ->> 她被困在雨中，要求搭乘路过的送货车。  

apart /əˈpɑrt/ from, 除了……以外。  
Apart from a few raindrops, the picnic went perfectly. ->> 除了一些雨滴外，野餐进行得很顺利。  
Apart from Ken, everyone remembered to bring their passport. ->> 除了Ken之外，每个人都记得带护照。  

As /ɛz/ I soon learnt, he was English himself! 我很快就知道，他自己就是个英国人。  
As I soon learnt, the new neighbour had grown up just three streets away from me. ->> 我很快了解到，新邻居的成长地点离我只有三条街。  
As I soon learnt, the mysterious caller was actually my cousin playing a joke. ->> 我很快了解到，那个神秘的来电者实际上是我表弟在开玩笑。
