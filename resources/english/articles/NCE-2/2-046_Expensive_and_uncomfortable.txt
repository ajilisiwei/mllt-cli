When a plane from London arrived at Sydney airport, workers began to unload a number of wooden boxes which contained clothing. ->> 当一架来自伦敦的飞机抵达悉尼机场时，工人们开始卸下装有服装的一批木箱。

No one could account for the fact that one of the boxes was extremely heavy. ->> 其中有只箱子特别重，可谁也弄不清是怎么回事。

It suddenly occurred to one of the workers to open up the box. ->> 突然一个工人想到打开箱子看看。

He was astonished at what he found. ->> 看到的情景使他吃惊。

A man was lying in the box on top of a pile of woolen goods. ->> 箱内有一个人正躺在一堆毛织品之上。

He was so surprised at being discovered that he did not even try to run away. ->> 他由于被人发现而感到非常吃惊，甚至都没有企图逃跑。

After he was arrested, the man admitted hiding in the box before the plane left London. ->> 此人被逮捕后，承认他是在飞机离开伦敦前躲进箱里的。

He had had a long and uncomfortable trip, for he had been confined to the wooden box for over eighteen hours. ->> 他经历了一次漫长而又难受的旅程，因为他在那木箱里闷了18个多小时。

The man was ordered to pay $3,500 for the cost of the trip. ->> 此人被责令交付旅费3,500英镑。

The normal price of a ticket is $2,000! ->> 而正常票价是2,000英镑！

The workers were ready to unload the shipment when the truck arrived. ->> 卡车到达时，工人们正准备卸货。  
It took the team an hour to unload all the boxes from the delivery. ->> 团队花了一个小时才把所有的箱子从送货上卸下来。

She admired the intricate carvings on the wooden door. ->> 她欣赏木门上精美的雕刻。  

extremely /ɪkˈstrimli/ adv. 非常，极其
He was extremely happy to hear the good news. ->> 听到这个好消息，他非常高兴。  
The movie was extremely well-received by both critics and audiences. ->> 这部电影受到评论家和观众的一致好评。  

occur /əˈkər/ v. 发生
The event is expected to occur sometime next week. ->> 该活动预计将于下周某个时间举行。  
Strange phenomena often occur in that region of the sea. ->> 那个海域经常发生奇怪的现象。  

astonish /əˈstɑnɪʃ/ v. 使惊讶
Her sudden decision to move abroad astonished everyone. ->> 她突然决定出国，让所有人都感到惊讶。  
He was astonished by the magician's incredible performance. ->> 他对魔术师不可思议的表演感到惊讶。  

pile /paɪl/ n. 堆
There was a pile of clothes on the chair that needed folding. ->> 椅子上有一堆需要折叠的衣服。  
He built a bonfire with a large pile of wood. ->> 他用一大堆木头生了一堆篝火。  

woollen /ˈwʊlən/ adj. 羊毛的
She wore a cozy woollen sweater to keep warm in the winter. ->> 她穿着一件舒适的羊毛衫以在冬天保暖。  
The shop sold beautiful woollen scarves in various colors. ->> 这家商店出售各种颜色的漂亮羊毛围巾。  

goods /gʊdz/ n. （常用复数）货物，商品
The ship docked to unload its cargo of goods from overseas. ->> 该船靠岸卸载来自海外的货物。  
Customers were eager to see the new goods displayed in the store. ->> 顾客们迫不及待地想要看到店里展示的新商品。

Scientists hope to discover new cures through their research. ->> 科学家希望通过他们的研究发现新的治疗方法。  
She was surprised to discover an old letter hidden in the book. ->> 她惊讶地发现书里藏着一封旧信。

She finally admitted her feelings to her best friend. ->> 她终于向她最好的朋友承认了自己的感情。

Due to the storm, they had to confine themselves to the cabin for safety. ->> 由于暴风雨，为了安全起见，他们不得不将自己限制在船舱内。  

normal /ˈnɔrməl/ adj. 正常的，通常的
After a few days of rest, he felt back to his normal self. ->> 经过几天的休息，他感觉自己已经恢复正常了。
