We can read of things that happened 5,000 years ago in the Near East, where people first learned to write. ->> 我们从书籍中可读到5,000 年前近东发生的事情，那里的人最早学会了写字。  
But there are some parts of the world where even now people cannot write. ->> 但直到现在，世界上有些地方， 人们还不会书写。  
The only way that they can preserve their history is to recount it as sagas -- legends handed down from one generation of story teller to another. ->> 他们保存历史的唯一办法是将其作为传奇故事口述，由讲述人一代接一代地传下来。  
These legends are useful because they can tell us something about migrations of people who lived long ago, but none could write down what they did. ->> 这些传说很有用，因为它们可以告诉我们关于很久以前的迁徙故事，但当时的人们无法将这些记录下来。  
Anthropologists wondered where the remote ancestors of the Polynesian peoples now living in the Pacific Islands came from. ->> 人类学家想知道如今生活在太平洋群岛上的波利尼西亚人的远古祖先来自何方。  
The sagas of these people explain that some of them came from Indonesia about 2,000 years ago. ->> 当地人的传说解释说，他们中的一些人大约在2,000年前从印度尼西亚迁来。  
But the first people who were like ourselves lived so long ago that even their sagas, if they had any, are forgotten. ->> 但是，与我们相似的最早人类生活得久远，甚至他们的传说，即便有，也已被遗忘。  
So archaeologists have neither history nor legends to help them to find out where the first 'modern men' came from. ->> 因此，考古学家既没有历史记录，也没有传说来帮助他们找出最早的“现代人”来自哪里。  
Fortunately, however, ancient men made tools of stone, especially flint, because this is easier to shape than other kinds. ->> 然而，幸运的是，远古人用石头制作了工具，尤其是用燧石，因为这种石头比其他种类更容易成形。  
They may also have used wood and skins, but these have rotted away. ->> 他们可能也用过木材和兽皮，但这些东西已腐烂殆尽。  
Stone does not decay, and so the tools of long ago have remained when even the bones of the men who made them have disappeared without trace. ->> 石头不会腐烂，因此，尽管制造这些工具的人的骨头早已荡然无存，但远古时代的石头工具却保存了下来。
